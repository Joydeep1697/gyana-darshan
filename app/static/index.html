A disciplined method

From question to cited answer, without the black box.

The system exposes its sources and verification status—not private chain-of-thought—so every material conclusion can be checked.

01 / Retrieve

Find the governing text

Queries are mapped to relevant statutes, sections, savings clauses, and transition provisions.

02 / Route

Separate the timelines

Substantive liability, procedure, and evidence law are analysed on their own commencement rules.

03 / Reason

Answer within authority

The generation layer is constrained by the retrieved evidence pack and the question actually asked.

04 / Verify

Check every material claim

A legal claim firewall tests citations and can correct the answer before it reaches the user.
