Support

I cannot sign in

Check your email and password, then try signing in again. If your session expired, sign in before retrying a protected action. Google sign-in appears only when it is configured. This interface does not currently offer self-service password recovery.

My PDF did not upload or summarize

Use a PDF file you are authorized to upload. Check the error shown in the Vault, confirm your connection and workspace permissions, and retry. Scanned pages may not contain extractable text. A provider failure can prevent a summary even after upload succeeds.

A consultation failed or my allowance ran out

Read the displayed error before retrying. Check your remaining daily allowance and avoid submitting the same question repeatedly. Save a copy of an unfinished question before refreshing the page.

An answer looks wrong

Check the cited original text and the dates and facts you supplied. The presence of a citation does not prove a conclusion. Record not-helpful feedback and obtain independent review before relying on the answer.
