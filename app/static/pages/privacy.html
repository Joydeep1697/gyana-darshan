Privacy information

Understand the data
behind your workspace.

This page describes the application's current data handling. A complete operator privacy policy, including the responsible entity, contact details and retention periods, has not yet been published.

Account and research data

The application stores account details, saved consultations, messages, feedback and document metadata. Uploaded PDFs are retained in the deployment's document storage. Authentication tokens are held in browser storage to maintain your session.

Organization membership determines who can access shared workspace records. Check the selected workspace before uploading sensitive material.

AI and payment providers

Questions, relevant conversation context and selected document text may be sent to the configured AI provider to generate a response. Provider processing is subject to that provider's own terms.

When enabled, checkout uses Razorpay. The application records order, payment and activation information. Do not include passwords, payment credentials or unnecessary personal information in research prompts.

Controls and retention

You can delete consultations and Vault documents through the workspace. Operational audit records and backups may have separate retention. Deleting a visible item should not be assumed to erase every retained copy immediately.

A self-service account deletion flow and a published operator request process are not currently available here. See Contact & Support for any contact channel configured for this deployment.

Before sharing sensitive material

Confirm your authority to upload the material and understand your organization's rules. Minimize personal data and review the provider and deployment arrangements before submitting confidential documents.

This page makes no certification or compliance claim. The operator must publish its complete privacy policy before treating this information as a production privacy notice.
