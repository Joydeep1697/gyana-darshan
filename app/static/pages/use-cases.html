Questions, documents and a retained record.

The product connects statutory retrieval, saved consultations and PDF research. The examples below are demonstrations of a workflow, not client engagements or evidence of legal accuracy.

Illustrative workflow / 01

A matter spanning a statutory change

  1. Provide: the alleged conduct date, investigation start date and current stage.
  2. Ask: which substantive, procedural and evidence-law questions need separate analysis.
  3. Inspect: cited sections, excerpts and any missing-fact clarification.
  4. Review: commencement and savings provisions before relying on a conclusion.
Illustrative workflow / 02

Prepare a document review

  1. Provide: PDFs you are authorized to upload.
  2. Ask: a specific question across up to three selected documents.
  3. Inspect: the page excerpts attached to the answer.
  4. Review: the original pages and any omissions before using the summary.
