01 / Our story

Start with the text. Keep the questions open.

Indian legal research often requires moving between statutes, commencement dates, procedural rules and the documents in front of you. Nyaya Darshana brings those activities into one workspace, with persistent consultations and a Knowledge Vault for PDF documents.

The name reflects a commitment to reasoning and clear understanding. That commitment shapes the product: source excerpts stay visible, citation checks have explicit limits, and uncertain conclusions call for independent review.
