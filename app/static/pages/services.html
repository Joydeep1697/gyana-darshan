01 / Consultation

Ask with context.

Describe the legal issue and relevant facts in plain language. Continue a saved conversation, inspect cited provisions, and retain the answer for later review.

  • Saved consultation history and follow-up questions.
  • Source cards and an evidence status for each answer.
  • Markdown and Word exports for further work.

02 / Transition research

Keep the timelines distinct.

Research questions involving IPC/BNS, CrPC/BNSS and IEA/BSA. Provide the dates of conduct, investigation and proceedings so the system can separate the questions rather than assume a single governing date.

Missing facts may require clarification. Retrieved text and automated citation checks do not independently establish a legal conclusion.

03 / Knowledge Vault

Work from your PDFs.

Upload a PDF, inspect its metadata and request a summary. Select up to three owned documents to ask a focused question and review page-level excerpts.

  • Document listing and metadata search.
  • Cached summaries and document selection.
  • Authenticated access and document deletion.
Open Knowledge Vault
04 / Team workspaces

Keep shared work in scope.

Create an organization workspace, switch between workspaces, and manage membership in Account & access. Permissions distinguish owners, administrators, members and read-only viewers.

Review workspace membership before uploading sensitive material. Members may have access to the documents and consultations in that workspace according to their role.
