Frequently asked questions

Do I need to pay to start?

No payment is required to create an account and begin within its displayed allowance.

Does an activation buy unlimited queries?

No. Query limits are enforced by the server according to account role. Payment activation currently records a plan without changing those limits.

Is the payment a monthly subscription?

The current checkout records access activation. It does not establish a recurring subscription term. Review the details shown before completing a payment.

Is this legal advice or a lawyer appointment?

It is research software. The product does not arrange representation or determine case outcomes. Use a qualified professional when your situation requires legal advice.

What if I need help with my account or PDFs?

Visit Support for sign-in, quota and document troubleshooting.
