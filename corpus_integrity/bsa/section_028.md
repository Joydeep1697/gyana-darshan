# BSA Section 28: Entries in books of account when relevant

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 28
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Entries in books of account when relevant.—Entries in the books of account, including those maintained in an electronic form, regularly kept in the c

to charge any person with liability. 
Illustration. 
A sues B for one thousand rupees, and shows entries in his account books showing B to be indebted 
to him to this amount. The entries are relevant, but are not sufficient, without other evidence, to prove the 
debt.
