# BSA Section 120: Presumption as to absence of consent in certain prosecution for rape

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 120
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Presumption as to absence of consent in certain prosecution for rape.—In a prosecution for rape under sub-section (2) of section 64 of the Bharatiya

have been raped and such woman states in her evidence before the Court that she did not consent, the 
Court shall presume that she did not consent. 
Explanation.—In this section, “sexual intercourse” shall mean any of the acts mentioned in 
section 63 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.
