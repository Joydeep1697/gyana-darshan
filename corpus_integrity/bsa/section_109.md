# BSA Section 109: Burden of proving fact especially within knowledge

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 109
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Burden of proving fact especially within knowledge.—When any fact is especially within the knowledge of any person, the burden of proving that fact i

(a) When a person does an act with some intention other than that which the character and 
circumstances of the act suggest, the burden of proving that intention is upon him. 
(b) A is charged with travelling on a railway without a ticket. The burden of proving that he had a 
ticket is on him.
