# BSA Section 20: When oral admissions as to contents of documents are relevant

- Short name: BSA
- Section: 20
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

When oral admissions as to contents of documents are relevant.—Oral admissions as to the contents of a document are not relevant, unless and until th

contained, or unless the genuineness of a document produced is in question.
