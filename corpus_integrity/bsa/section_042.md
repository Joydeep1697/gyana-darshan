# BSA Section 42: Opinion as to existence of general custom or right, when relevant

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 42
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Opinion as to existence of general custom or right, when relevant.—When the Court has to form an opinion as to the existence of any general custom or

Explanation.—The expression “general custom or right” includes customs or rights common to any 
considerable class of persons. 
Illustration. 
The right of the villagers of a particular village to use the water of a particular well is a general right 
within the meaning of this section.
