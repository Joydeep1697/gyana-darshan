# BSA Section 70: Proof when attesting witness denies execution

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 70
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Proof when attesting witness denies execution.—If the attesting witness denies or does not recollect the execution of the document, its execution may

law to be attested may be proved as if it was unattested.
