# BSA Section 97: Exclusion of evidence against application of document to existing facts

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 97
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Exclusion of evidence against application of document to existing facts.—When language used in a document is plain in itself, and when it applies acc

Illustration. 
A sells to B, by deed, “my estate at Rampur containing one hundred bighas”. A has an estate at 
Rampur containing one hundred bighas. Evidence may not be given of the fact that the estate meant to be 
sold was one situated at a different place and of a different size.
