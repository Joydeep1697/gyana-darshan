# BSA Section 76: Proof of documents by production of certified copies

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 76
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Proof of documents by production of certified copies.—Such certified copies may be produced in proof of the contents of the public documents or parts
