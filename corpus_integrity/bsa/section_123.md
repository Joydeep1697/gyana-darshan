# BSA Section 123: Estoppel of acceptor of bill of exchange, bailee or licensee

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 123
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Estoppel of acceptor of bill of exchange, bailee or licensee.—No acceptor of a bill of exchange shall be permitted to deny that the drawer had author

bailment or licence commenced, authority to make such bailment or grant such licence. 
Explanation 1.—The acceptor of a bill of exchange may deny that the bill was really drawn by the 
person by whom it purports to have been drawn. 
Explanation 2.—If a bailee delivers the goods bailed to a person other than the bailor, he may prove 
that such person had a right to them as against the bailor.
