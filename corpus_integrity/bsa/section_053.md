# BSA Section 53: Facts admitted need not be proved

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 53
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Facts admitted need not be proved.—No fact needs to be proved in any proceeding which the parties thereto or their agents agree to admit at the heari

deemed to have admitted by their pleadings: 
Provided that the Court may, in its discretion, require the facts admitted to be proved otherwise than 
by such admissions. 
CHAPTER IV 
OF ORAL EVIDENCE
