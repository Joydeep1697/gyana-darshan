# BSA Section 62: Special provisions as to evidence relating to electronic record

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 62
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Special provisions as to evidence relating to electronic record

62. Special provisions as to evidence relating to electronic record.—The contents of electronic records may be proved in accordance with the provisions of section 63.
