# BSA Section 98: Evidence as to document unmeaning in reference to existing facts

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 98
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Evidence as to document unmeaning in reference to existing facts.—When language used in a document is plain in itself, but is unmeaning in reference

38 
 
Illustration. 
A sells to B, by deed, “my house in Kolkata”. A had no house in Kolkata, but it appears that he had a 
house at Howrah, of which B had been in possession since the execution of the deed. These facts may be 
proved to show that the deed related to the house at Howrah.
