# BSA Section 104: Burden of proof

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 104
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Burden of proof.—Whoever desires any Court to give judgment as to any legal right or liability dependent on the existence of facts which he asserts m

39 
 
Illustrations. 
(a) A desires a Court to give judgment that B shall be punished for a crime which A says B has 
committed. A must prove that B has committed the crime. 
(b) A desires a Court to give judgment that he is entitled to certain land in the possession of B, by 
reason of facts which he asserts, and which B denies, to be true. A must prove the existence of those 
facts.
