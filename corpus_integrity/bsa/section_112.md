# BSA Section 112: Burden of proof as to relationship in the cases of partners, landlord and tenant, principal and agent

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 112
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Burden of proof as to relationship in the cases of partners, landlord and tenant, principal and agent.—When the question is whether persons are partn

stand, or have ceased to stand, to each other in those relationships respectively, is on the person who 
affirms it.
