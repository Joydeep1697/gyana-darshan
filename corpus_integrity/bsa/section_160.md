# BSA Section 160: Former statements of witness may be proved to corroborate later testimony as to same fact

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 160
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Former statements of witness may be proved to corroborate later testimony as to same fact.—In order to corroborate the testimony of a witness, any fo

legally competent to investigate the fact, may be proved.
