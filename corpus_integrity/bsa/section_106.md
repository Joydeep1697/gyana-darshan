# BSA Section 106: Burden of proof as to particular fact

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 106
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Burden of proof as to particular fact.—The burden of proof as to any particular fact lies on that person who wishes the Court to believe in its exist

Illustration. 
A prosecutes B for theft, and wishes the Court to believe that B admitted the theft to C. A must prove 
the admission. B wishes the Court to believe that, at the time in question, he was elsewhere. He must 
prove it.
