# BSA Section 49: Previous bad character not relevant, except in reply

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 49
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Previous bad character not relevant, except in reply.—In criminal proceedings, the fact that the accused has a bad character, is irrelevant, unless e

Explanation 1.—This section does not apply to cases in which the bad character of any person is 
itself a fact in issue. 
Explanation 2.—A previous conviction is relevant as evidence of bad character.
