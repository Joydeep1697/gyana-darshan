# BSA Section 131: Information as to commission of offences

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 131
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Information as to commission of offences.—No Magistrate or police officer shall be compelled to say when he got any information as to the commission

against the public revenue. 
Explanation.—“revenue officer” means any officer employed in or about the business of any branch 
of the public revenue.
