# BSA Section 67: Proof of execution of document required by law to be attested

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 67
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Proof of execution of document required by law to be attested.—If a document is required by law to be attested, it shall not be used as evidence unti

Court and capable of giving evidence: 
Provided that it shall not be necessary to call an attesting witness in proof of the execution of any 
document, not being a will, which has been registered in accordance with the provisions of the Indian 
Registration Act, 1908 (16 of 1908), unless its execution by the person by whom it purports to have been 
executed is specifically denied.
