# BSA Section 89: Presumption as to books, maps and charts

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 89
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Presumption as to books, maps and charts.—The Court may presume that any book to which it may refer for information on matters of public or general i

published by the person, and at the time and place, by whom or at which it purports to have been written 
or published.
