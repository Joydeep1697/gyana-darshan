# BSA Section 96: Exclusion of evidence to explain or amend ambiguous document

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 96
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Exclusion of evidence to explain or amend ambiguous document.—When the language used in a document is, on its face, ambiguous or defective, evidence

Illustrations. 
(a) A agrees, in writing, to sell a horse to B for “one lakh rupees or one lakh fifty thousand rupees”. 
Evidence cannot be given to show which price was to be given. 
(b) A deed contains blanks. Evidence cannot be given of facts which would show how they were 
meant to be filled.
