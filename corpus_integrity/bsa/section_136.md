# BSA Section 136: Production of documents or electronic records which another person, having possession, could refuse to produce

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 136
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Production of documents or electronic records which another person, having possession, could refuse to produce.—No one shall be compelled to produce

were in his possession or control, unless such last-mentioned person consents to their production.
