# BSA Section 93: Presumption as to electronic records five years old

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 93
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Presumption as to electronic records five years old.—Where any electronic record, purporting or proved to be five years old, is produced from any cus

signature of any particular person was so affixed by him or any person authorised by him in this behalf. 
Explanation.—The Explanation to section 81 shall also apply to this section.
