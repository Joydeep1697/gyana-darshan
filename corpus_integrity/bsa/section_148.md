# BSA Section 148: Cross-examination as to previous statements in writing

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 148
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Cross-examination as to previous statements in writing.—A witness may be cross-examined as to previous statements made by him in writing or reduced i
