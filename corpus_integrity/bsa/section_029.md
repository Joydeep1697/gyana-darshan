# BSA Section 29: Relevancy of entry in public record or an electronic record made in performance of duty

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 29
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Relevancy of entry in public record or an electronic record made in performance of duty.— An entry in any public or other official book, register or r

person in performance of a duty specially enjoined by the law of the country in which such book, register 
or record or an electronic record, is kept, is itself a relevant fact.
