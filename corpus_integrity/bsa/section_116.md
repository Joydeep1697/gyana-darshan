# BSA Section 116: Birth during marriage, conclusive proof of legitimacy

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 116
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Birth during marriage, conclusive proof of legitimacy.—The fact that any person was born during the continuance of a valid marriage between his mothe

legitimate child of that man, unless it can be shown that the parties to the marriage had no access to each 
other at any time when he could have been begotten.
