# BSA Section 73: Proof as to verification of digital signature

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 73
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Proof as to verification of digital signature.—In order to ascertain whether a digital signature is that of the person by whom it purports to have be

Certificate; 
(b) any other person to apply the public key listed in the Digital Signature Certificate and verify 
the digital signature purported to have been affixed by that person.
