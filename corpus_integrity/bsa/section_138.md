# BSA Section 138: Accomplice

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 138
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Accomplice.—An accomplice shall be a competent witness against an accused person; and a conviction is not illegal if it proceeds upon the corroborate

proof of any fact. 
CHAPTER X 
OF EXAMINATION OF WITNESSES
