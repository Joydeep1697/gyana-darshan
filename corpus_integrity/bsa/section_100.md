# BSA Section 100: Evidence as to application of language to one of two sets of facts, to neither of which the whole correctly applies

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 100
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Evidence as to application of language to one of two sets of facts, to neither of which the whole correctly applies.—When the language used applies p

given to show to which of the two it was meant to apply. 
Illustration. 
A agrees to sell to B “my land at X in the occupation of Y”. A has land at X, but not in the 
occupation of Y, and he has land in the occupation of Y but it is not at X. Evidence may be given of facts 
showing which he meant to sell.
