# BSA Section 84: Presumption as to powers-of-attorney

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 84
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Presumption as to powers-of-attorney.—The Court shall presume that every document purporting to be a power-of-attorney, and to have been executed bef

Government, was so executed and authenticated.
