# BSA Section 18: Admissions by persons expressly referred to by party to suit

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 18
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Admissions by persons expressly referred to by party to suit.—Statements made by persons to whom a party to the suit has expressly referred for infor

Illustration. 
The question is, whether a horse sold by A to B is sound. 
A says to B— “Go and ask C, C knows all about it”. C's statement is an admission.
