# BSA Section 133: Privilege not waived by volunteering evidence

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 133
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Privilege not waived by volunteering evidence.—If any party to a suit gives evidence therein at his own instance or otherwise, he shall not be deemed

witness, he shall be deemed to have consented to such disclosure only if he questions such advocate, on 
matters which, but for such question, he would not be at liberty to disclose.
