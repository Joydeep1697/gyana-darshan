# BSA Section 163: Testimony to facts stated in document mentioned in section 162

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 163
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Testimony to facts stated in document mentioned in section 162.—A witness may also testify to facts mentioned in any such document as is mentioned in

document. 
Illustration. 
A book-keeper may testify to facts recorded by him in books regularly kept in the course of business, 
if he knows that the books were correctly kept, although he has forgotten the particular transactions 
entered.
