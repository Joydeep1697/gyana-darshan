# BSA Section 43: Opinion as to usages, tenets, etc

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 43
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Opinion as to usages, tenets, etc., when relevant.—When the Court has to form an opinion as to— (i) the usages and tenets of any body of men or fami

(ii) the constitution and governance of any religious or charitable foundation; or 
(iii) the meaning of words or terms used in particular districts or by particular classes of people, 
the opinions of persons having special means of knowledge thereon, are relevant facts.
