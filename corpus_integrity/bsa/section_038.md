# BSA Section 38: Fraud or collusion in obtaining judgment, or incompetency of Court, may be proved

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 38
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Fraud or collusion in obtaining judgment, or incompetency of Court, may be proved.—Any party to a suit or other proceeding may show that any judgment

competent to deliver it, or was obtained by fraud or collusion.
