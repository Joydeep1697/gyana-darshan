# BSA Section 169: No new trial for improper admission or rejection of evidence

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 169
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

No new trial for improper admission or rejection of evidence.—The improper admission or rejection of evidence shall not be ground of itself for a new

evidence objected to and admitted, there was sufficient evidence to justify the decision, or that, if the 
rejected evidence had been received, it ought not to have varied the decision.
