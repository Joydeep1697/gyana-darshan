# BSA Section 101: Evidence as to meaning of illegible characters, etc

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 101
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Evidence as to meaning of illegible characters, etc.—Evidence may be given to show the meaning of illegible or not commonly intelligible characters,

Illustration. 
A, sculptor, agrees to sell to B, “all my mods”. A has both models and modelling tools. Evidence 
may be given to show which he meant to sell.
