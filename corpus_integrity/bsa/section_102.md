# BSA Section 102: Who may give evidence of agreement varying terms of document

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 102
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Who may give evidence of agreement varying terms of document.—Persons who are not parties to a document, or their representatives in interest, may gi

Illustration. 
A and B make a contract in writing that B shall sell A certain cotton, to be paid for on delivery. At 
the same time, they make an oral agreement that three months’ credit shall be given to A. This could not 
be shown as between A and B, but it might be shown by C, if it affected his interests.
