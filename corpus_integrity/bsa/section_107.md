# BSA Section 107: Burden of proving fact to be proved to make evidence admissible

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 107
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Burden of proving fact to be proved to make evidence admissible.—The burden of proving any fact necessary to be proved in order to enable any person

Illustrations. 
(a) A wishes to prove a dying declaration by B. A must prove B's death. 
(b) A wishes to prove, by secondary evidence, the contents of a lost document. A must prove that the 
document has been lost.
