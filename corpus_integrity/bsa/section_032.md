# BSA Section 32: Relevancy of statements as to any law contained in law books including electronic or digital form

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 32
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Relevancy of statements as to any law contained in law books including electronic or digital form.—When the Court has to form an opinion as to a law

authority of the Government of such country and to contain any such law, and any report of a ruling of 
the Courts of such country contained in a book including in electronic or digital form purporting to be a 
report of such rulings, is relevant.
