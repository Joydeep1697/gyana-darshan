# BSA Section 134: Confidential communication with legal advisers

- Short name: BSA
- Section: 134
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Confidential communication with legal advisers.—No one shall be compelled to disclose to the Court any confidential communication which has taken pla
