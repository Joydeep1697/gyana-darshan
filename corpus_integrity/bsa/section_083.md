# BSA Section 83: Presumption as to collections of laws and reports of decisions

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 83
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Presumption as to collections of laws and reports of decisions.—The Court shall presume the genuineness of, every book purporting to be printed or pu

reports of decisions of the Courts of such country.
