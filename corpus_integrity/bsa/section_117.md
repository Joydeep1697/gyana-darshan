# BSA Section 117: Presumption as to abetment of suicide by a married woman

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 117
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Presumption as to abetment of suicide by a married woman.—When the question is whether the commission of suicide by a woman had been abetted by her h

marriage and that her husband or such relative of her husband had subjected her to cruelty, the Court may 
presume, having regard to all the other circumstances of the case, that such suicide had been abetted by 
her husband or by such relative of her husband. 
Explanation.—For the purposes of this section, “cruelty” shall have the same meaning as in 
section 86 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.
