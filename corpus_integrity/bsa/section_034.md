# BSA Section 34: Previous judgments relevant to bar a second suit or trial

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 34
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Previous judgments relevant to bar a second suit or trial.—The existence of any judgment, order or decree which by law prevents any Court from taking

such trial.
