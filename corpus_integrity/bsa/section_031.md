# BSA Section 31: Relevancy of statement as to fact of public nature contained in certain Acts or notifications

- Statute: Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
- Short name: BSA
- Section: 31
- Act number: Act 47 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 55, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Relevancy of statement as to fact of public nature contained in certain Acts or notifications.—When the Court has to form an opinion as to the existe

Government or State Government notification appearing in the respective Official Gazette or in any 
printed paper or in electronic or digital form purporting to be such Gazette, is a relevant fact.
