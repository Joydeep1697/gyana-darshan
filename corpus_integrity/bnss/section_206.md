# BNSS Section 206: High Court to decide, in case of doubt, district where inquiry or trial shall take place

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 206
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

High Court to decide, in case of doubt, district where inquiry or trial shall take place.— Where two or more Courts have taken cognizance of the same

(a) if the Courts are subordinate to the same High Court, by that High Court; 
(b) if the Courts are not subordinate to the same High Court, by the High Court within the local 
limits of whose appellate criminal jurisdiction the proceedings were first commenced, and thereupon 
all other proceedings in respect of that offence shall be discontinued.
