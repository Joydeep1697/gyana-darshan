# BNSS Section 191: Complainant and witnesses not to be required to accompany police officer and not to be subject to restraint

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 191
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Complainant and witnesses not to be required to accompany police officer and not to be subject to restraint.— No complainant or witness on his way to

security for his appearance other than his own bond: 
Provided that if any complainant or witness refuses to attend or to execute a bond as directed in section 
190, the officer in charge of the police station may forward him in custody to the Magistrate, who may 
detain him in custody until he executes such bond, or until the hearing of the case is completed.
