# BNSS Section 15: Special Executive Magistrates

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 15
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Special Executive Magistrates.—The State Government may appoint, for such term as it may think fit, Executive Magistrates or any police officer not b

particular functions and confer on such Special Executive Magistrates such of the powers as are conferrable 
under this Sanhita on Executive Magistrates, as it may deem fit.
