# BNSS Section 382: Procedure of Magistrate taking cognizance

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 382
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Procedure of Magistrate taking cognizance.—(1) A Magistrate to whom a complaint is made under section 379 or section 380 shall, notwithstanding anyth

(2) Where it is brought to the notice of such Magistrate, or of any other Magistrate to whom the case 
may have been transferred, that an appeal is pending against the decision arrived at in the judicial 
proceeding out of which the matter has arisen, he may, if he thinks fit, at any stage, adjourn the hearing of 
the case until such appeal is decided.
