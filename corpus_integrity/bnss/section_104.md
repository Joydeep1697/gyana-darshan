# BNSS Section 104: Disposal of things found in search beyond jurisdiction

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 104
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Disposal of things found in search beyond jurisdiction.—When, in the execution of a search- warrant at any place beyond the local jurisdiction of the

provisions hereinafter contained, shall be immediately taken before the Court issuing the warrant, unless 
such place is nearer to the Magistrate having jurisdiction therein than to such Court, in which case the list 
and things shall be immediately taken before such Magistrate; and, unless there be good cause to the 
contrary, such Magistrate shall make an order authorising them to be taken to such Court.
