# BNSS Section 161: Injunction pending inquiry

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 161
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Injunction pending inquiry.—(1) If a Magistrate making an order under section 152 considers that immediate measures should be taken to prevent immine

obviate or prevent such danger or injury pending the determination of the matter. 
(2) In default of such person forthwith obeying such injunction, the Magistrate may himself use, or 
cause to be used, such means as he thinks fit to obviate such danger or to prevent such injury. 
(3) No suit shall lie in respect of anything done in good faith by a Magistrate under this section.
