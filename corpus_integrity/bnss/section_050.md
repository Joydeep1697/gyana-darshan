# BNSS Section 50: Power to seize offensive weapons

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 50
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Power to seize offensive weapons.—The police officer or other person making any arrest under this Sanhita may, immediately after the arrest is made,

or whom the officer or person making the arrest is required by this Sanhita to produce the person arrested.
