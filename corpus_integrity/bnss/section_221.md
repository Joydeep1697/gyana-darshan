# BNSS Section 221: Cognizance of offence

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 221
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Cognizance of offence.— No Court shall take cognizance of an offence punishable under section 67 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita,

or made by the wife against the husband.
