# BNSS Section 493: Procedure in case of insolvency or death of surety or when a bond is forfeited

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 493
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Procedure in case of insolvency or death of surety or when a bond is forfeited.—When any surety to a bail bond under this Sanhita becomes insolvent o

may order the person from whom such security was demanded to furnish fresh security in accordance with 
the directions of the original order, and if such security is not furnished, such Court or Magistrate may 
proceed as if there had been a default in complying with such original order.
