# BNSS Section 387: Discharge of offender on submission of apology

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 387
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Discharge of offender on submission of apology.—When any Court has under section 384 adjudged an offender to punishment, or has under section 385 for

interruption, the Court may, in its discretion, discharge the offender or remit the punishment on his 
submission to the order or requisition of such Court, or on apology being made to its satisfaction.
