# BNSS Section 505: Power to sell perishable property

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 505
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Power to sell perishable property.—If the person entitled to the possession of such property is unknown or absent and the property is subject to spee

property is less than ten thousand rupees, the Magistrate may at any time direct it to be sold; and the 
provisions of sections 503 and 504 shall, as nearly as may be practicable, apply to the net proceeds of such 
sale.
