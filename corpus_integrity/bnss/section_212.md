# BNSS Section 212: Making over of cases to Magistrates

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 212
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Making over of cases to Magistrates.—(1) Any Chief Judicial Magistrate may, after taking cognizance of an offence, make over the case for inquiry or

(2) Any Magistrate of the first class empowered in this behalf by the Chief Judicial Magistrate may, 
after taking cognizance of an offence, make over the case for inquiry or trial to such other competent 
Magistrate as the Chief Judicial Magistrate may, by general or special order, specify, and thereupon such 
Magistrate may hold the inquiry or trial.
