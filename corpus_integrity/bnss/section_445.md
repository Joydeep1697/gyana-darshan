# BNSS Section 445: High Court's order to be certified to lower Court

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 445
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

High Court's order to be certified to lower Court.—When a case is revised under this Chapter by the High Court or a Sessions Judge, it or he shall, i

the Court to which the decision or order is so certified shall thereupon make such orders as are conformable 
to the decision so certified, and, if necessary, the record shall be amended in accordance therewith.
