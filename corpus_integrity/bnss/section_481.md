# BNSS Section 481: Bail to require accused to appear before next Appellate Court

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 481
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Bail to require accused to appear before next Appellate Court.—(1) Before conclusion of the trial and before disposal of the appeal, the Court trying

such Court issues notice in respect of any appeal or petition filed against the judgment of the respective 
Court and such bond shall be in force for six months. 
(2) If such accused fails to appear, the bond stand forfeited and the procedure under section 491 shall 
apply.
