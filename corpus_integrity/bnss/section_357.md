# BNSS Section 357: Procedure where accused does not understand proceedings

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 357
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Procedure where accused does not understand proceedings.—If the accused, though not a person of unsound mind, cannot be made to understand the procee

the proceedings shall be forwarded to the High Court with a report of the circumstances of the case, and the 
High Court shall pass thereon such order as it thinks fit.
