# BNSS Section 76: Warrant directed to police officer

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 76
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Warrant directed to police officer.—A warrant directed to any police officer may also be executed by any other police officer whose name is endorsed
