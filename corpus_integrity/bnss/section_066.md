# BNSS Section 66: Service when persons summoned cannot be found

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 66
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Service when persons summoned cannot be found.—Where the person summoned cannot, by the exercise of due diligence, be found, the summons may be serve

so left shall, if so required by the serving officer, sign a receipt therefor on the back of the other duplicate. 
Explanation.—A servant is not a member of the family within the meaning of this section.
