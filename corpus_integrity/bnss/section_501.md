# BNSS Section 501: Destruction of libellous and other matter

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 501
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Destruction of libellous and other matter.—(1) On a conviction under section 294, section 295, or sub-sections (3) and (4) of section 356 of the Bhar

custody of the Court or remain in the possession or power of the person convicted. 
(2) The Court may, in like manner, on a conviction under section 274, section 275, section 276 
or section 277 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, order the food, drink, drug or medical preparation in 
respect of which the conviction was had, to be destroyed.
