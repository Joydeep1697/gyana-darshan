# BNSS Section 331: Affidavit in proof of conduct of public servants

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 331
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Affidavit in proof of conduct of public servants.—When any application is made to any Court in the course of any inquiry, trial or other proceeding u

116 
 
respecting any public servant, the applicant may give evidence of the facts alleged in the application by 
affidavit, and the Court may, if it thinks fit, order that evidence relating to such facts be so given.
