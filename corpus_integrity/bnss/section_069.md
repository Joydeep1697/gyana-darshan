# BNSS Section 69: Service of summons outside local limits

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 69
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Service of summons outside local limits.—When a Court desires that a summons issued by it shall be served at any place outside its local jurisdiction
