# BNSS Section 412: Procedure in cases submitted to High Court for confirmation

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 412
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Procedure in cases submitted to High Court for confirmation.—In cases submitted by the Court of Session to the High Court for the confirmation of a s

Court, send either physically, or through electronic means, a copy of the order, under the seal of the High 
Court and attested with his official signature, to the Court of Session.
