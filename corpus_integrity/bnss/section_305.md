# BNSS Section 305: Prisoner to be brought to Court in custody

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 305
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Prisoner to be brought to Court in custody.—Subject to the provisions of section 304, the officer in charge of the prison shall, upon delivery of an

order to be taken to the Court in which his attendance is required, so as to be present there at the time 
mentioned in the order, and shall cause him to be kept in custody in or near the Court until he has been 
examined or until the Court authorises him to be taken back to the prison in which he was confined or 
detained.
