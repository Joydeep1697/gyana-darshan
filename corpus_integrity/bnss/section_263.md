# BNSS Section 263: Framing of charge

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 263
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Framing of charge.—(1) If, upon such consideration, examination, if any, and hearing, the Magistrate is of opinion that there is ground for presuming

punished by him, he shall frame in writing a charge against the accused within a period of sixty days from 
the date of first hearing on charge. 

101 
 
(2) The charge shall then be read and explained to the accused, and he shall be asked whether he pleads 
guilty of the offence charged or claims to be tried.
