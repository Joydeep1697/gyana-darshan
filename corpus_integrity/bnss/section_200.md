# BNSS Section 200: Place of trial where act is an offence by reason of relation to other offence

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 200
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Place of trial where act is an offence by reason of relation to other offence.—When an act is an offence by reason of its relation to any other act w

by a Court within whose local jurisdiction either act was done.
