# BNSS Section 499: Payment to innocent purchaser of money found on accused

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 499
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Payment to innocent purchaser of money found on accused.—When any person is convicted of any offence which includes, or amounts to, theft or receivin

same was stolen, and that any money has on his arrest been taken out of the possession of the convicted 
person, the Court may, on the application of such purchaser and on the restitution of the stolen property to 
the person entitled to the possession thereof, order that out of such money a sum not exceeding the price 
paid by such purchaser be delivered to him within six months from the date of such order.
