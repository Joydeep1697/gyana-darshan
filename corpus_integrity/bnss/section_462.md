# BNSS Section 462: Effect of such warrant

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 462
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Effect of such warrant.—A warrant issued under clause (a) of sub-section (1) of section 461 by any Court may be executed within the local jurisdictio

whose local jurisdiction such property is found.
