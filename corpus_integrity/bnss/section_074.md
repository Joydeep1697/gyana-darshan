# BNSS Section 74: Warrants to whom directed

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 74
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Warrants to whom directed.—(1) A warrant of arrest shall ordinarily be directed to one or more police officers; but the Court issuing such a warrant

shall execute the same. 
(2) When a warrant is directed to more officers or persons than one, it may be executed by all, or by 
any one or more of them.
