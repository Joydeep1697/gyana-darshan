# BNSS Section 133: Copy of order to accompany summons or warrant

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 133
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Copy of order to accompany summons or warrant.—Every summons or warrant issued under section 132 shall be accompanied by a copy of the order made und

under, the same.
