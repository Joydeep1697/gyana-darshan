# BNSS Section 16: Local Jurisdiction of Executive

## Text

Local Jurisdiction of Executive.—(1) Subject to the control of the State Government, the District Magistrate may, from time to time, define the local

(2) Except as otherwise provided by such definition, the jurisdiction and powers of every such
