# BNSS Section 254: Evidence for prosecution

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 254
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Evidence for prosecution.— (1) On the date so fixed, the Judge shall proceed to take all such evidence as may be produced in support of the prosecuti

means. 
(2) The deposition of evidence of any public servant may be taken through audio-video electronic 
means. 
(3) The Judge may, in his discretion, permit the cross-examination of any witness to be deferred until 
any other witness or witnesses have been examined or recall any witness for further cross-examination.
