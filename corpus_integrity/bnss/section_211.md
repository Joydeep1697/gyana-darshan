# BNSS Section 211: Transfer on application of accused

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 211
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Transfer on application of accused.—When a Magistrate takes cognizance of an offence under clause (c) of sub-section (1) of section 210, the accused

the accused, if there be more than one, objects to further proceedings before the Magistrate taking 
cognizance, the case shall be transferred to such other Magistrate as may be specified by the Chief Judicial 
Magistrate in this behalf.
