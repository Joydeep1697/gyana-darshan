# BNSS Section 172: Persons bound to conform to lawful directions of police

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 172
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Persons bound to conform to lawful directions of police.— (1) All persons shall be bound to conform to the lawful directions of a police officer give

69 
 
(2) A police officer may detain or remove any person resisting, refusing, ignoring or disregarding to 
conform to any direction given by him under sub-section (1) and may either take such person before a 
Magistrate or, in petty cases, release him as soon as possible within a period of twenty-four hours.
