# BNSS Section 182: No inducement to be offered

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 182
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

No inducement to be offered.—(1) No police officer or other person in authority shall offer or make, or cause to be offered or made, any such inducem

(2) But no police officer or other person shall prevent, by any caution or otherwise, any person from 
making in the course of any investigation under this Chapter any statement which he may be disposed to 
make of his own free will: 
Provided that nothing in this sub-section shall affect the provisions of sub-section (4) of section 183.
