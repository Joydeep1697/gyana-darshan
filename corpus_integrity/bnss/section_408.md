# BNSS Section 408: Power to direct further inquiry to be made or additional evidence to be taken

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 408
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Power to direct further inquiry to be made or additional evidence to be taken.—(1) If, when such proceedings are submitted, the High Court thinks tha

may make such inquiry or take such evidence itself, or direct it to be made or taken by the Court of Session. 
(2) Unless the High Court otherwise directs, the presence of the convicted person may be dispensed 
with when such inquiry is made or such evidence is taken. 
(3) When the inquiry or evidence (if any) is not made or taken by the High Court, the result of such 
inquiry or evidence shall be certified to such Court.
