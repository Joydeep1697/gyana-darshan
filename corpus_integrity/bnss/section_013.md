# BNSS Section 13: Subordination of Judicial Magistrates

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 13
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Subordination of Judicial Magistrates.—(1) Every Chief Judicial Magistrate shall be subordinate to the Sessions Judge; and every other Judicial Magis

(2) The Chief Judicial Magistrate may, from time to time, make rules or give special orders, consistent 
with this Sanhita, as to the distribution of business among the Judicial Magistrates subordinate to him.
