# BNSS Section 252: Conviction on plea of guilty

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 252
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Conviction on plea of guilty.—If the accused pleads guilty, the Judge shall record the plea and may, in his discretion, convict him thereon.
