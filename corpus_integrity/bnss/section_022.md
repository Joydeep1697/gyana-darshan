# BNSS Section 22: Sentences which High Courts and Sessions Judges may pass

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 22
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Sentences which High Courts and Sessions Judges may pass.—(1) A High Court may pass any sentence authorised by law. (2) A Sessions Judge or Addition

sentence of death passed by any such Judge shall be subject to confirmation by the High Court.
