# BNSS Section 286: Record in summary trials

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 286
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Record in summary trials.—In every case tried summarily, the Magistrate shall enter, in such form as the State Government may direct, the following p

(b) the date of the commission of the offence; 
(c) the date of the report or complaint; 
(d) the name of the complainant (if any); 
(e) the name, parentage and residence of the accused; 
(f) the offence complained of and the offence (if any) proved, and in cases coming under clause (i), 
clause (ii) or clause (iii) of sub-section (1) of section 283, the value of the property in respect of which 
the offence has been committed; 
(g) the plea of the accused and his examination (if any); 
(h) the finding; 
(i) the sentence or other final order; 
(j) the date on which proceedings terminated.
