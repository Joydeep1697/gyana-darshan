# BNSS Section 209: Receipt of evidence relating to offences committed outside India

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 209
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Receipt of evidence relating to offences committed outside India.—When any offence alleged to have been committed in a territory outside India is bei

produced, either in physical form or in electronic form, before a judicial officer, in or for that territory or 
before a diplomatic or consular representative of India in or for that territory shall be received as evidence 
by the Court holding such inquiry or trial in any case in which such Court might issue a commission for 
taking evidence as to the matters to which such depositions or exhibits relate.
