# BNSS Section 309: Record in summonscases and inquiries

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 309
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Record in summonscases and inquiries.— (1) In all summons-cases tried before a Magistrate, in all inquiries under sections 164 to 167 (both inclusive

make a memorandum of the substance of the evidence in the language of the Court: 
Provided that if the Magistrate is unable to make such memorandum himself, he shall, after recording 
the reason of his inability, cause such memorandum to be made in writing or from his dictation in open 
Court. 
(2) Such memorandum shall be signed by the Magistrate and shall form part of the record.
