# BNSS Section 344: Power to direct tender of pardon

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 344
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Power to direct tender of pardon.—At any time after commitment of a case but before judgment is passed, the Court to which the commitment is made may

tender a pardon on the same condition to such person.
