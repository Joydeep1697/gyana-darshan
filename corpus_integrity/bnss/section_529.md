# BNSS Section 529: Duty of High Court to exercise continuous superintendence over Courts

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 529
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Duty of High Court to exercise continuous superintendence over Courts.—Every High Court shall so exercise its superintendence over the Courts of Sess

Magistrates.
