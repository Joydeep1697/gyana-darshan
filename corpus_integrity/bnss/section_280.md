# BNSS Section 280: Withdrawal of complaint

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 280
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Withdrawal of complaint.—If a complainant, at any time before a final order is passed in any case under this Chapter, satisfies the Magistrate that t

the Magistrate may permit him to withdraw the same, and shall thereupon acquit the accused against whom 
the complaint is so withdrawn.
