# BNSS Section 56: Health and safety of arrested person

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 56
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Health and safety of arrested person.—It shall be the duty of the person having the custody of an accused to take reasonable care of the health and s

officer making an arrest without warrant shall, without unnecessary delay and subject to the provisions 
herein contained as to bail, take or send the person arrested before a Magistrate having jurisdiction in the 
case, or before the officer in charge of a police station.
