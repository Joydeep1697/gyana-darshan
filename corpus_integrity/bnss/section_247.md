# BNSS Section 247: Withdrawal of remaining charges on conviction on one of several charges

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 247
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Withdrawal of remaining charges on conviction on one of several charges.—When a charge containing more heads than one is framed against the same pers

the Court, withdraw the remaining charge or charges, or the Court of its own accord may stay the inquiry 
into, or trial of, such charge or charges and such withdrawal shall have the effect of an acquittal on such 
charge or charges, unless the conviction be set aside, in which case the said Court (subject to the order of 
the Court setting aside the conviction) may proceed with the inquiry into, or trial of, the charge or charges 
so withdrawn.
