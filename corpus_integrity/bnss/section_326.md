# BNSS Section 326: Deposition of medical witness

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 326
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Deposition of medical witness.—(1) The deposition of a civil surgeon or other medical witness, taken and attested by a Magistrate in the presence of

deponent is not called as a witness. 
(2) The Court may, if it thinks fit, and shall, on the application of the prosecution or the accused, 
summon and examine any such deponent as to the subject-matter of his deposition.
