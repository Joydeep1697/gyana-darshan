# BNSS Section 297: Period of detention undergone by accused to be set off against sentence of imprisonment

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 297
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Period of detention undergone by accused to be set off against sentence of imprisonment.— The provisions of section 468 shall apply, for setting off t

respect of the imprisonment under other provisions of this Sanhita.
