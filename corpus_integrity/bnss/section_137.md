# BNSS Section 137: Discharge of person informed against

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 137
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Discharge of person informed against.—If, on an inquiry under section 135, it is not proved that it is necessary for keeping the peace or maintaining

record to that effect, and if such person is in custody only for the purposes of the inquiry, shall release him, 
or, if such person is not in custody, shall discharge him.
