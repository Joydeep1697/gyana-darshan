# BNSS Section 288: Language of record and judgment

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 288
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Language of record and judgment.—(1) Every such record and judgment shall be written in the language of the Court. (2) The High Court may authorise

aforesaid record or judgment or both by means of an officer appointed in this behalf by the Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, and the record or judgment so prepared shall be signed by such Magistrate.
