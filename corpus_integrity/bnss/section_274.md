# BNSS Section 274: Substance of accusation to be stated

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 274
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Substance of accusation to be stated.—When in a summons-case the accused appears or is brought before the Magistrate, the particulars of the offence

frame a formal charge: 
Provided that if the Magistrate considers the accusation as groundless, he shall, after recording reasons 
in writing, release the accused and such release shall have the effect of discharge.
