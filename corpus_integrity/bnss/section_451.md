# BNSS Section 451: Making over or withdrawal of cases by Executive Magistrates

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 451
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Making over or withdrawal of cases by Executive Magistrates.—Any District Magistrate or Sub-divisional Magistrate may— (a) make over, for disposal,

subordinate to him; 
(b) withdraw any case from, or recall any case which he has made over to, any Magistrate subordinate 
to him, and dispose of such proceeding himself or refer it for disposal to any other Magistrate.
