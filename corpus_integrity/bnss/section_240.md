# BNSS Section 240: Recall of witnesses when charge altered

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 240
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Recall of witnesses when charge altered.—Whenever a charge is altered or added to by the Court after the commencement of the trial, the prosecutor an

who may have been examined, unless the Court, for reasons to be recorded in writing, considers that 
the prosecutor or the accused, as the case may be, desires to recall or re-examine such witness for the 
purpose of vexation or delay or for defeating the ends of justice; 
(b) also to call any further witness whom the Court may think to be material. 
B.—Joinder of charges
