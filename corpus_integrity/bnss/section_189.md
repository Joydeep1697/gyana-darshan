# BNSS Section 189: Release of accused when evidence deficient

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 189
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Release of accused when evidence deficient.—If, upon an investigation under this Chapter, it appears to the officer in charge of the police station t

custody, release him on his executing a bond or bail bond, as such officer may direct, to appear, if and when 
so required, before a Magistrate empowered to take cognizance of the offence on a police report, and to try 
the accused or commit him for trial.
