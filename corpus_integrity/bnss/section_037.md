# BNSS Section 37: Designated police officer

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 37
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Designated police officer.—The State Government shall— (a) establish a police control room in every district and at State level; (b) designate a pol

Assistant Sub-Inspector of Police who shall be responsible for maintaining the information about the 
names and addresses of the persons arrested, nature of the offence with which charged, which shall be 
prominently displayed in any manner including in digital mode in every police station and at the district 
headquarters.
