# BNSS Section 68: Service on Government servant

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 68
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Service on Government servant.—(1) Where the person summoned is in the active service of the Government, the Court issuing the summons shall ordinari

manner provided by section 64, and shall return it to the Court under his signature with the endorsement 
required by that section. 
(2) Such signature shall be evidence of due service.
