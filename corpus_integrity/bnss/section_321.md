# BNSS Section 321: Execution of commissions

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 321
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Execution of commissions.—Upon receipt of the commission, the Chief Judicial Magistrate or such Magistrate as he may appoint in this behalf, shall su

exercise the same powers, as in trials of warrant-cases under this Sanhita.
