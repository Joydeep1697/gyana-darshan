# BNSS Section 41: Arrest by Magistrate

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 41
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Arrest by Magistrate.—(1) When any offence is committed in the presence of a Magistrate, whether Executive or Judicial, within his local jurisdiction

offender to custody. 
(2) Any Magistrate, whether Executive or Judicial, may at any time arrest or direct the arrest, in his 
presence, within his local jurisdiction, of any person for whose arrest he is competent at the time and in the 
circumstances to issue a warrant.
