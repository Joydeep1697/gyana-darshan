# BNSS Section 469: Saving

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 469
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Saving.—(1) Nothing in section 466 or section 467 shall be held to excuse any person from any part of the punishment to which he is liable upon his f

of imprisonment and the person undergoing the sentence is after its execution to undergo a further 
substantive sentence or further substantive sentences of imprisonment, effect shall not be given to the award 
of imprisonment in default of payment of the fine until the person has undergone the further sentence or 
sentences.
