# BNSS Section 153: Service or notification of order

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 153
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Service or notification of order.—(1) The order shall, if practicable, be served on the person against whom it is made, in the manner herein provided

the State Government may, by rules, direct, and a copy thereof shall be stuck up at such place or places as 
may be fittest for conveying the information to such person.
