# BNSS Section 348: Power to summon material witness, or examine person present

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 348
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Power to summon material witness, or examine person present.—Any Court may, at any stage of any inquiry, trial or other proceeding under this Sanhita

examined; and the Court shall summon and examine or re-call and re-examine any such person if his 
evidence appears to it to be essential to the just decision of the case.
