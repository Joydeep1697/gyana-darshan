# BNSS Section 362: Procedure when after commencement of inquiry or trial, Magistrate finds case should be committed

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 362
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Procedure when after commencement of inquiry or trial, Magistrate finds case should be committed.—If, in any inquiry into an offence or a trial befor

Session, he shall commit it to that Court under the provisions hereinbefore contained and thereupon the 
provisions of Chapter XIX shall apply to the commitment so made.
