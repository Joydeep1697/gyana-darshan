# BNSS Section 248: Trial to be conducted by Public Prosecutor

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 248
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Trial to be conducted by Public Prosecutor.—In every trial before a Court of Session, the prosecution shall be conducted by a Public Prosecutor. 249

pursuance of a commitment of the case under section 232, or under any other law for the time being in 
force, the prosecutor shall open his case by describing the charge brought against the accused and stating 
by what evidence he proposes to prove the guilt of the accused.
