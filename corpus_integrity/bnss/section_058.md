# BNSS Section 58: Person arrested not to be detained more than twenty-four hours

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 58
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Person arrested not to be detained more than twenty-four hours.—No police officer shall detain in custody a person arrested without warrant for a lon

exceed twenty-four hours exclusive of the time necessary for the journey from the place of arrest to the 
Magistrate's Court, whether having jurisdiction or not.
