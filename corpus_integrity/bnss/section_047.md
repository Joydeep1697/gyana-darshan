# BNSS Section 47: Person arrested to be informed of grounds of arrest and of right to bail

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 47
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Person arrested to be informed of grounds of arrest and of right to bail.—(1) Every police officer or other person arresting any person without warra

(2) Where a police officer arrests without warrant any person other than a person accused of a non-
bailable offence, he shall inform the person arrested that he is entitled to be released on bail and that he 
may arrange for sureties on his behalf.
