# BNSS Section 504: Procedure where no claimant appears within six months

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 504
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Procedure where no claimant appears within six months.—(1) If no person within such period establishes his claim to such property, and if the person

shall be at the disposal of the State Government and may be sold by that Government and the proceeds of 
such sale shall be dealt with in such manner as the State Government may, by rules, provide. 

169 
 
(2) An appeal shall lie against any such order to the Court to which appeals ordinarily lie from 
convictions by the Magistrate.
