# BNSS Section 67: Procedure when service cannot be effected as before provided

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 67
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Procedure when service cannot be effected as before provided.—If service cannot by the exercise of due diligence be effected as provided in section 6

which the person summoned ordinarily resides; and thereupon the Court, after making such inquiries as it 
thinks fit, may either declare that the summons has been duly served or order fresh service in such manner 
as it considers proper.
