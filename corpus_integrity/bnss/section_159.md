# BNSS Section 159: Power of Magistrate to furnish written instructions, etc

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 159
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Power of Magistrate to furnish written instructions, etc.—(1) Where the Magistrate directs a local investigation by any person under section 158, the

(b) declare by whom the whole or any part of the necessary expenses of the local investigation shall 
be paid. 
(2) The report of such person may be read as evidence in the case. 
(3) Where the Magistrate summons and examines an expert under section 158, the Magistrate may 
direct by whom the costs of such summoning and examination shall be paid.
