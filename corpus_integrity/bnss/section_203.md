# BNSS Section 203: Offence committed on journey or voyage

- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 203
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Offence committed on journey or voyage.—When an offence is committed whilst the person by or against whom, or the thing in respect of which, the offe

jurisdiction that person or thing passed in the course of that journey or voyage.
