# BNSS Section 406: Court of Session to send copy of finding and sentence to District Magistrate

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 406
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Court of Session to send copy of finding and sentence to District Magistrate.—In cases tried by the Court of Session or a Chief Judicial Magistrate,

jurisdiction the trial was held.
