# BNSS Section 323: Return of commission

- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 323
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Return of commission.—(1) After any commission issued under section 319 has been duly executed, it shall be returned, together with the deposition of
