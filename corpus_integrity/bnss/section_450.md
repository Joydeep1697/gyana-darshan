# BNSS Section 450: Withdrawal of cases by Judicial Magistrates

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 450
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Withdrawal of cases by Judicial Magistrates.—(1) Any Chief Judicial Magistrate may withdraw any case from, or recall any case which he has made over

competent to inquire into or try the same. 
(2) Any Judicial Magistrate may recall any case made over by him under sub-section (2) of section 212 
to any other Magistrate and may inquire into or try such cases himself.
