# BNSS Section 391: Certain Judges and Magistrates not to try certain offences when committed before themselves

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 391
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Certain Judges and Magistrates not to try certain offences when committed before themselves.—Except as provided in sections 383, 384, 388 and 389, no

when such offence is committed before himself or in contempt of his authority, or is brought under his 
notice as such Judge or Magistrate in the course of a judicial proceeding. 
CHAPTER XXIX 
THE JUDGMENT
