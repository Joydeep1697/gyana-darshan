# BNSS Section 306: Power to issue commission for examination of witness in prison

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 306
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Power to issue commission for examination of witness in prison.—The provisions of this Chapter shall be without prejudice to the power of the Court t

of Chapter XXV shall apply in relation to the examination on commission of any such person in the prison 
as they apply in relation to the examination on commission of any other person. 
CHAPTER XXV 
EVIDENCE IN INQUIRIES AND TRIALS
