# BNSS Section 314: Interpretation of evidence to accused or his advocate

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 314
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Interpretation of evidence to accused or his advocate.—(1) Whenever any evidence is given in a language not understood by the accused, and he is pres

(2) If he appears by an advocate and the evidence is given in a language other than the language of the 
Court, and not understood by the advocate, it shall be interpreted to such advocate in that language. 
(3) When documents are put for the purpose of formal proof, it shall be in the discretion of the Court 
to interpret as much thereof as appears necessary.
