# BNSS Section 416: No appeal in certain cases when accused pleads guilty

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 416
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

No appeal in certain cases when accused pleads guilty.—Notwithstanding anything in section 415, where an accused person has pleaded guilty and has be

(i) if the conviction is by a High Court; or 
(ii) if the conviction is by a Court of Session or Magistrate of the first or second class, except as to 
the extent or legality of the sentence.
