# BNSS Section 518: Continuing offence

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 518
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Continuing offence.—In the case of a continuing offence, a fresh period of limitation shall begin to run at every moment of the time during which the

foregoing provisions of this Chapter, any Court may take cognizance of an offence after the expiry of the 
period of limitation, if it is satisfied on the facts and in the circumstances of the case that the delay has been 
properly explained or that it is necessary so to do in the interests of justice.
