# BNSS Section 62: Arrest to be made strictly according to Sanhita

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 62
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Arrest to be made strictly according to Sanhita.— No arrest shall be made except in accordance with the provisions of this Sanhita or any other law f

PROCESSES TO COMPEL APPEARANCE 
A.—Summons
