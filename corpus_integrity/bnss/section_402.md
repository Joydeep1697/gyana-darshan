# BNSS Section 402: Special reasons to be recorded in certain cases

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 402
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Special reasons to be recorded in certain cases.—Where in any case the Court could have dealt with,— (a) an accused person under section 401 or unde

1958 (20 of 1958); or 
(b) a youthful offender under the Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015 
(2 of 2016) or any other law for the time being in force for the treatment, training or rehabilitation of 
youthful offenders, 
but has not done so, it shall record in its judgment the special reasons for not having done so.
