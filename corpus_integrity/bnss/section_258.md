# BNSS Section 258: Judgment of acquittal or conviction

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 258
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Judgment of acquittal or conviction.—(1) After hearing arguments and points of law (if any), the Judge shall give a judgment in the case, as soon as

in writing. 
(2) If the accused is convicted, the Judge shall, unless he proceeds in accordance with the provisions of 
section 401, hear the accused on the questions of sentence, and then pass sentence on him according to law.
