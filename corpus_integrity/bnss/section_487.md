# BNSS Section 487: Discharge from custody

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 487
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Discharge from custody.—(1) As soon as the bond or bail bond has been executed, the person for whose appearance it has been executed shall be release

orders shall release him. 
(2) Nothing in this section, section 478 or section 480, shall be deemed to require the release of any 
person liable to be detained for some matter other than that in respect of which the bond or bail bond was 
executed.
