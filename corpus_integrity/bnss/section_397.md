# BNSS Section 397: Treatment of victims

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 397
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Treatment of victims.—All hospitals, public or private, whether run by the Central Government, the State Government, local bodies or any other person

66, section 67, section 68, section 70, section 71 or sub-section (1) of section 124 of the Bharatiya Nyaya 
Sanhita, 2023 (45 of 2023) or under sections 4, 6, 8 or section 10 of the Protection of Children from Sexual 
Offences Act, 2012 (32 of 2012), and shall immediately inform the police of such incident.
