# BNSS Section 428: Judgments of subordinate Appellate Court

- Statute: Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNSS
- Section: 428
- Act number: Act 46 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 54, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Judgments of subordinate Appellate Court.—The rules contained in Chapter XXIX as to the judgment of a Criminal Court of original jurisdiction shall a

Provided that, unless the Appellate Court otherwise directs, the accused shall not be brought up, or 
required to attend, to hear judgment delivered.
