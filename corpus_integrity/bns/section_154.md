# BNS Section 154: Whoever commits depredation, or makes preparations to commit depredation, on the territories of any foreign State at peace with the Government of Indi

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 154
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever commits depredation, or makes preparations to commit depredation, on the territories of any foreign State at peace with the Government of Indi

also be liable to fine and to forfeiture of any property used or intended to be used in
committing such depredation, or acquired by such depredation.
