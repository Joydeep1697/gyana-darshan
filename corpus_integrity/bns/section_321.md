# BNS Section 321: Whoever dishonestly or fraudulently prevents any debt or demand due to himself or to any other person from being made available according to law for p

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 321
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever dishonestly or fraudulently prevents any debt or demand due to himself or to any other person from being made available according to law for p

for a term which may extend to two years, or with fine, or with both.
