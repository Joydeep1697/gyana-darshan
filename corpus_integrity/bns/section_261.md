# BNS Section 261: Whoever, being a public servant legally bound as such public servant to keep in confinement any person charged with or convicted of any offence or law

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 261
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, being a public servant legally bound as such public servant to keep in confinement any person charged with or convicted of any offence or law

with simple imprisonment for a term which may extend to two years, or with fine, or with
both.
