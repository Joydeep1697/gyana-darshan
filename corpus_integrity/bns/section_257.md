# BNS Section 257: Whoever, being a public servant, corruptly or maliciously makes or pronounces in any stage of a judicial proceeding, any report, order, verdict, or de

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 257
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, being a public servant, corruptly or maliciously makes or pronounces in any stage of a judicial proceeding, any report, order, verdict, or de

a term which may extend to seven years, or with fine, or with both.
