# BNS Section 136: Whoever assaults or uses criminal force to any person on grave and sudden provocation given by that person, shall be punished with simple imprisonment

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 136
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever assaults or uses criminal force to any person on grave and sudden provocation given by that person, shall be punished with simple imprisonment

with both.
Explanation.—This section is subject to the same Explanation as section 131.
