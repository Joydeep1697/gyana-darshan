# BNS Section 232: (1) Whoever threatens another with any injury to his person, reputation or property or to the person or reputation of any one in whom that person is i

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 232
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

(1) Whoever threatens another with any injury to his person, reputation or property or to the person or reputation of any one in whom that person is i

either description for a term which may extend to seven years, or with fine, or with both.
(2) If innocent person is convicted and sentenced in consequence of false evidence
referred to in sub-section (1), with death or imprisonment for more than seven years, the
person who threatens shall be punished with the same punishment and sentence in the
same manner and to the same extent such innocent person is punished and sentenced.
