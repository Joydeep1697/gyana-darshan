# BNS Section 194: (1) When two or more persons, by fighting in a public place, disturb the public peace, they are said to commit an affray

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 194
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

(1) When two or more persons, by fighting in a public place, disturb the public peace, they are said to commit an affray. (2)Whoever commitsanaffray,s

for a term which may extend to one month, or with fine which may extend to one thousand
rupees, or with both.
