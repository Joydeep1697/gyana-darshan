# BNS Section 247: Whoever fraudulently obtains a decree or order against any person for a sum not due, or for a larger sum than is due or for any property or interest i

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 247
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever fraudulently obtains a decree or order against any person for a sum not due, or for a larger sum than is due or for any property or interest i

person after it has been satisfied or for anything in respect of which it has been satisfied,
or fraudulently suffers or permits any such act to be done in his name, shall be punished
with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to two years, or with
fine, or with both.
