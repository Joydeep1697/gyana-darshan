# BNS Section 148: Whoever within or without and beyond India conspires to commit any of the offences punishable by section 147, or conspires to overawe, by means of cri

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 148
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever within or without and beyond India conspires to commit any of the offences punishable by section 147, or conspires to overawe, by means of cri

with imprisonment for life, or with imprisonment of either description which may extend to
ten years, and shall also be liable to fine.
Explanation.—To constitute a conspiracy under this section, it is not necessary that
any act or illegal omission shall take place in pursuance thereof.
