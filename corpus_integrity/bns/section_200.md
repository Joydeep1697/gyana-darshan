# BNS Section 200: Whoever, being in charge of a hospital, public or private, whether run by the Central Government, the State Government, local bodies or any other pers

## Text

Whoever, being in charge of a hospital, public or private, whether run by the Central Government, the State Government, local bodies or any other pers

punished with imprisonment for a term which may extend to one year, or with fine, or with
