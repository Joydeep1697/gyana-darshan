# BNS Section 340: (1) A false document or electronic record made wholly or in part by forgery is designated a forged document or electronic record

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 340
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

(1) A false document or electronic record made wholly or in part by forgery is designated a forged document or electronic record. (2) Whoever fraudule

record which he knows or has reason to believe to be a forged document or electronic record,
shall be punished in the same manner as if he had forged such document or electronic record.
