# BNS Section 156: Whoever, being a public servant and having the custody of any State prisoner or prisoner of war, voluntarily allows such prisoner to escape from any p

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 156
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, being a public servant and having the custody of any State prisoner or prisoner of war, voluntarily allows such prisoner to escape from any p

description for a term which may extend to ten years, and shall also be liable to fine.
