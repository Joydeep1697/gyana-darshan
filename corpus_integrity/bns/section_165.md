# BNS Section 165: The master or person in charge of a merchant vessel, on board of which any deserter from the Army, Navy or Air Force of the Government of India is con

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 165
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

The master or person in charge of a merchant vessel, on board of which any deserter from the Army, Navy or Air Force of the Government of India is con

rupees, if he might have known of such concealment but for some neglect of his duty as such
master or person in charge, or but for some want of discipline on board of the vessel.
