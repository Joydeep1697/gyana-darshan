# BNS Section 159: Whoever abets the committing of mutiny by an officer, soldier, sailor or airman, in the Army, Navy or Air Force of the Government of India or attempts

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 159
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever abets the committing of mutiny by an officer, soldier, sailor or airman, in the Army, Navy or Air Force of the Government of India or attempts

imprisonment for life, or with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend
to ten years, and shall also be liable to fine.
