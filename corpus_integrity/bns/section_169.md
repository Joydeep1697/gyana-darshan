# BNS Section 169: For the purposes of this Chapter— (a) “candidate” means a person who has been nominated as a candidate at any election;

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 169
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

For the purposes of this Chapter— (a) “candidate” means a person who has been nominated as a candidate at any election;

(b) “electoral right” means the right of a person to stand, or not to stand as, or to
withdraw from being, a candidate or to vote or refrain from voting at an election.
