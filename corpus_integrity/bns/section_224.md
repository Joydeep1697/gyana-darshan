# BNS Section 224: Whoever holds out any threat of injury to any public servant, or to any person in whom he believes that public servant to be interested, for the purpo

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 224
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever holds out any threat of injury to any public servant, or to any person in whom he believes that public servant to be interested, for the purpo

exercise of the public functions of such public servant, shall be punished with imprisonment
of either description for a term which may extend to two years, or with fine, or with both.
