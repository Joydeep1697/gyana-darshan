# BNS Section 149: Whoever collects men, arms or ammunition or otherwise prepares to wage war with the intention of either waging or being prepared to wage war against t

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 149
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever collects men, arms or ammunition or otherwise prepares to wage war with the intention of either waging or being prepared to wage war against t

a term not exceeding ten years, and shall also be liable to fine.
