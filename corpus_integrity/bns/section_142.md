# BNS Section 142: Whoever, knowing that any person has been kidnapped or has been abducted, wrongfully conceals or confines such person, shall be punished in the same m

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 142
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, knowing that any person has been kidnapped or has been abducted, wrongfully conceals or confines such person, shall be punished in the same m

same purpose as that with or for which he conceals or detains such person in confinement.
