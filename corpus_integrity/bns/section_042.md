# BNS Section 42: If the offence, the committing of which, or the attempting to commit which occasions the exercise of the right of private defence, be theft, mischief,

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 42
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

If the offence, the committing of which, or the attempting to commit which occasions the exercise of the right of private defence, be theft, mischief,

causing of death, but does extend, subject to the restrictions specified in section 37, to the
voluntary causing to the wrong-doer of any harm other than death.
