# BNS Section 225: Whoever holds out any threat of injury to any person for the purpose of inducing that person to refrain or desist from making a legal application for

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 225
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever holds out any threat of injury to any person for the purpose of inducing that person to refrain or desist from making a legal application for

or to cause such protection to be given, shall be punished with imprisonment of either
description for a term which may extend to one year, or with fine, or with both.
