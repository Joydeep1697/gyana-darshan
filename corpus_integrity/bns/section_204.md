# BNS Section 204: Whoever pretends to hold any particular office as a public servant, knowing that he does not hold such office or falsely personates any other person h

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 204
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever pretends to hold any particular office as a public servant, knowing that he does not hold such office or falsely personates any other person h

be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which shall not be less than
six months but which may extend to three years and with fine.
