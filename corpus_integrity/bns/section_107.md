# BNS Section 107: If any child, any person of unsound mind, any delirious person or any person in a state of intoxication, commits suicide, whoever abets the commission

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 107
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

If any child, any person of unsound mind, any delirious person or any person in a state of intoxication, commits suicide, whoever abets the commission

years, and shall also be liable to fine.
