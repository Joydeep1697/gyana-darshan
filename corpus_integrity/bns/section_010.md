# BNS Section 10: In all cases in which judgment is given that a person is guilty of one of several offences specified in the judgment, but that it is doubtful of which

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 10
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

In all cases in which judgment is given that a person is guilty of one of several offences specified in the judgment, but that it is doubtful of which

provided if the same punishment is not provided for all.
