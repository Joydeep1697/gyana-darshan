# BNS Section 239: Whoever, knowing or having reason to believe that an offence has been committed, intentionally omits to give any information respecting that offence w

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 239
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, knowing or having reason to believe that an offence has been committed, intentionally omits to give any information respecting that offence w

which may extend to six months, or with fine which may extend to five thousand rupees,
or with both.
