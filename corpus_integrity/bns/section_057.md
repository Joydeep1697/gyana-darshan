# BNS Section 57: Whoever abets the commission of an offence by the public generally or by any number or class of persons exceeding ten, shall be punished with imprison

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 57
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever abets the commission of an offence by the public generally or by any number or class of persons exceeding ten, shall be punished with imprison

Illustration.
A affixes in a public place a placard instigating a sect consisting of more than ten
members to meet at a certain time and place, for the purpose of attacking the members of an
adverse sect, while engaged in a procession. A has committed the offence defined in this
section.
