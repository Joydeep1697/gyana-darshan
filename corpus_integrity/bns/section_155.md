# BNS Section 155: Whoever receives any property knowing the same to have been taken in the commission of any of the offences mentioned in sections 153 and 154, shall be

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 155
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever receives any property knowing the same to have been taken in the commission of any of the offences mentioned in sections 153 and 154, shall be

be liable to fine and to forfeiture of the property so received.
