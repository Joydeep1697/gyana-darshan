# BNS Section 94: Whoever, by secretly burying or otherwise disposing of the dead body of a child whether such child die before or after or during its birth, intentiona

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 94
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, by secretly burying or otherwise disposing of the dead body of a child whether such child die before or after or during its birth, intentiona

for a term which may extend to two years, or with fine, or with both.
