# BNS Section 357: Whoever, being bound by a lawful contract to attend on or to supply the wants of any person who, by reason of youth, or of unsoundness of mind, or of

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 357
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, being bound by a lawful contract to attend on or to supply the wants of any person who, by reason of youth, or of unsoundness of mind, or of

wants, voluntarilyomits so to do, shall be punished with imprisonment ofeither description
for a term which mayextend to three months, or with fine which mayextend tofive thousand
rupees, or with both.
