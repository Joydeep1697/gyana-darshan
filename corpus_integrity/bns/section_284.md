# BNS Section 284: Whoever knowingly or negligently conveys, or causes to be conveyed for hire, any person by water in any vessel, when that vessel is in such a state or

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 284
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever knowingly or negligently conveys, or causes to be conveyed for hire, any person by water in any vessel, when that vessel is in such a state or

for a term which may extend to six months, or with fine which may extend to five thousand
rupees, or with both.
