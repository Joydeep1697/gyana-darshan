# BNS Section 23: Nothing is an offence which is done by a person who, at the time of doing it, is, by reason of intoxication, incapable of knowing the nature of the ac

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 23
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Nothing is an offence which is done by a person who, at the time of doing it, is, by reason of intoxication, incapable of knowing the nature of the ac

administered to him without his knowledge or against his will.
