# BNS Section 339: Whoever has in his possession any document or electronic record, knowing the same to be forged and intending that the same shall fraudulently or disho

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 339
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever has in his possession any document or electronic record, knowing the same to be forged and intending that the same shall fraudulently or disho

section 337 of this Sanhita, be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term
which may extend to seven years, and shall also be liable to fine; and if the document is one
of the description mentioned in section 338, shall be punished with imprisonment for life, or
with imprisonment of either description, for a term which may extend to seven years, and
shall also be liable to fine.
