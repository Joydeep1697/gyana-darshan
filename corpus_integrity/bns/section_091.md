# BNS Section 91: Whoever before the birth of any child does any act with the intention of thereby preventing that child from being born alive or causing it to die afte

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 91
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever before the birth of any child does any act with the intention of thereby preventing that child from being born alive or causing it to die afte

such act be not caused in good faith for the purpose of saving the life of the mother, be
punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to ten years,
or with fine, or with both.
