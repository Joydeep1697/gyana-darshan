# BNS Section 234: Whoever issues or signs any certificate required by law to be given or signed, or relating to any fact of which such certificate is by law admissible

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 234
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever issues or signs any certificate required by law to be given or signed, or relating to any fact of which such certificate is by law admissible

same manner as if he gave false evidence.
