# BNS Section 176: Whoever without the general or special authority in writing of a candidate incurs or authorises expenses on account of the holding of any public meeti

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 176
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever without the general or special authority in writing of a candidate incurs or authorises expenses on account of the holding of any public meeti

promoting or procuring the election of such candidate, shall be punished with fine which
may extend to ten thousand rupees:
Provided that if any person having incurred any such expenses not exceeding the
amount of ten rupees without authority obtains within ten days from the date on which such
expenses were incurred the approval in writing of the candidate, he shall be deemed to have
incurred such expenses with the authority of the candidate.
