# BNS Section 141: Whoever imports into India from any country outside India any girl under the age of twenty-one years or any boy under the age of eighteen years with i

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 141
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever imports into India from any country outside India any girl under the age of twenty-one years or any boy under the age of eighteen years with i

with another person, shall be punishable with imprisonment which may extend to ten years
and shall also be liable to fine.
