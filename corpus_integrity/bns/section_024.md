# BNS Section 24: In cases where an act done is not an offence unless done with a particular knowledge or intent, a person who does the act in a state of intoxication s

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 24
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

In cases where an act done is not an offence unless done with a particular knowledge or intent, a person who does the act in a state of intoxication s

the thing which intoxicated him was administered to him without his knowledge or against
his will.
