# BNS Section 220: Whoever, at any sale of property held by the lawful authority of a public servant, as such, purchases or bids for any property on account of any perso

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 220
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, at any sale of property held by the lawful authority of a public servant, as such, purchases or bids for any property on account of any perso

property at that sale, or bids for such property not intending to perform the obligations
under which he lays himself by such bidding, shall be punished with imprisonment of
either description for a term which may extend to one month, or with fine which may
extend to two hundred rupees, or with both.
