# BNS Section 236: Whoever, in any declaration made or subscribed by him, which declaration any Court or any public servant or other person, is bound or authorised by la

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 236
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, in any declaration made or subscribed by him, which declaration any Court or any public servant or other person, is bound or authorised by la

believes to be false or does not believe to be true, touching any point material to the
object for which the declaration is made or used, shall be punished in the same manner as
if he gave false evidence.
