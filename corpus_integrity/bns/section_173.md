# BNS Section 173: Whoever commits the offence of bribery shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to one year, or with fine

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 173
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever commits the offence of bribery shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to one year, or with fine

Explanation.—“Treating” means that form of bribery where the gratification consists
in food, drink, entertainment, or provision.
