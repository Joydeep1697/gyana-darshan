# BNS Section 218: Whoever offers any resistance to the taking of any property by the lawful authority of any public servant, knowing or having reason to believe that he

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 218
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever offers any resistance to the taking of any property by the lawful authority of any public servant, knowing or having reason to believe that he

may extend to six months, or with fine which may extend to ten thousand rupees, or with
both.
