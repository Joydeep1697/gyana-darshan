# BNS Section 258: Whoever, being in any office which gives him legal authority to commit persons for trial or to confinement, or to keep persons in confinement, corrupt

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 258
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, being in any office which gives him legal authority to commit persons for trial or to confinement, or to keep persons in confinement, corrupt

exercise of that authority knowing that in so doing he is acting contrary to law, shall be
punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to seven
years, or with fine, or with both.
