# BNS Section 215: Whoever refuses to sign any statement made by him, when required to sign that statement by a public servant legally competent to require that he shall

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 215
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever refuses to sign any statement made by him, when required to sign that statement by a public servant legally competent to require that he shall

three months, or with fine which may extend to three thousand rupees, or with both.
