# BNS Section 221: Whoever voluntarily obstructs any public servant in the discharge of his public functions, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description f

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 221
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever voluntarily obstructs any public servant in the discharge of his public functions, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description f

five hundred rupees, or with both.
