# BNS Section 275: Whoever sells, or offers or exposes for sale, as food or drink, any article which has been rendered or has become noxious, or is in a state unfit for

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 275
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever sells, or offers or exposes for sale, as food or drink, any article which has been rendered or has become noxious, or is in a state unfit for

imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to six months, or with fine
which may extend to five thousand rupees, or with both.
