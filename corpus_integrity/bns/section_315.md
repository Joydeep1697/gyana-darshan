# BNS Section 315: Whoever dishonestly misappropriates or converts to his own use any property, knowing that such property was in the possession of a deceased person at

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 315
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever dishonestly misappropriates or converts to his own use any property, knowing that such property was in the possession of a deceased person at

such possession, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which
may extend to three years, and shall also be liable to fine, and if the offender at the time of
such person’s decease was employed by him as a clerk or servant, the imprisonment may
extend to seven years.
Illustration.
Z dies in possession of furniture and money. His servant A, before the money comes
into the possession of any person entitled to such possession, dishonestly misappropriates
it. A has committed the offence defined in this section.
