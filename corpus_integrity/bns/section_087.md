# BNS Section 87: Whoever kidnaps or abducts any woman with intent that she may be compelled, or knowing it to be likely that she will be compelled, to marry any person

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 87
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever kidnaps or abducts any woman with intent that she may be compelled, or knowing it to be likely that she will be compelled, to marry any person

she will be forced or seduced to illicit intercourse, shall be punished with imprisonment of
either description for a term which may extend to ten years, and shall also be liable to fine;
and whoever, by means of criminal intimidation as defined in this Sanhita or of abuse of
authority or any other method of compulsion, induces any woman to go from any place with
intent that she may be, or knowing that it is likely that she will be, forced or seduced to illicit
intercourse with another person shall also be punishable as aforesaid.
Of causing miscarriage, etc.
