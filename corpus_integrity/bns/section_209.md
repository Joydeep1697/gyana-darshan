# BNS Section 209: Whoever fails to appear at the specified place and the specified time as required by a proclamation published under sub-section (1) of section 84 of t

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 209
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever fails to appear at the specified place and the specified time as required by a proclamation published under sub-section (1) of section 84 of t

three years, or with fine, or with both, or with community service, and where a declaration has
been made under sub-section (4) of that section pronouncing him as a proclaimed offender,
he shall be punished with imprisonment for a term which may extend to seven years and shall
also be liable to fine.
