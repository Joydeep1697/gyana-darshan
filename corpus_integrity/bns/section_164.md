# BNS Section 164: Whoever, except as hereinafter excepted, knowing or having reason to believe that an officer, soldier, sailor or airman, in theArmy, Navy orAir Force

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 164
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, except as hereinafter excepted, knowing or having reason to believe that an officer, soldier, sailor or airman, in theArmy, Navy orAir Force

imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to two years, or with fine or
with both.
Exception.—This provision does not extend to the case in which the harbour is given
by the spouse of the deserter.
