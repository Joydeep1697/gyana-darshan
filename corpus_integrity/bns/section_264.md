# BNS Section 264: Whoever, being a public servant legally bound as such public servant to apprehend, or to keep in confinement, any person in any case not provided for

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 264
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, being a public servant legally bound as such public servant to apprehend, or to keep in confinement, any person in any case not provided for

that person or suffers him to escape from confinement, shall be punished—
(a) if he does so intentionally, with imprisonment of either description for a term
which may extend to three years, or with fine, or with both; and
(b) if he does so negligently, with simple imprisonment for a term which may
extend to two years, or with fine, or with both.
