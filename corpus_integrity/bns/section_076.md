# BNS Section 76: Whoever assaults or uses criminal force to any woman or abets such act with the intention of disrobing or compelling her to be naked, shall be punishe

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 76
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever assaults or uses criminal force to any woman or abets such act with the intention of disrobing or compelling her to be naked, shall be punishe

seven years, and shall also be liable to fine.
