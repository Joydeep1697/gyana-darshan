# BNS Section 267: Whoever, intentionally offers any insult, or causes any interruption to any public servant, while such public servant is sitting in any stage of a jud

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 267
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, intentionally offers any insult, or causes any interruption to any public servant, while such public servant is sitting in any stage of a jud

which may extend to five thousand rupees, or with both.
