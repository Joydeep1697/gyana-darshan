# BNS Section 184: Whoever, fraudulently or with intent to cause loss to the Government, uses for any purpose a stamp issued by Government for the purpose of revenue, wh

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 184
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, fraudulently or with intent to cause loss to the Government, uses for any purpose a stamp issued by Government for the purpose of revenue, wh

which may extend to two years, or with fine, or with both.
