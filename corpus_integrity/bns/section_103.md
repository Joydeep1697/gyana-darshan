# BNS Section 103: (1) Whoever commits murder shall be punished with death or imprisonment for life, and shall also be liable to fine

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 103
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

(1) Whoever commits murder shall be punished with death or imprisonment for life, and shall also be liable to fine. (2) When a group of five or more p

ground of race, caste or community, sex, place of birth, language, personal belief or any

104.Whoever, being under sentence of imprisonment for life, commits murder, shall be
punished with death or with imprisonment for life, which shall mean the remainder of that
person’s natural life.
