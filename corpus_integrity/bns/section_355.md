# BNS Section 355: Whoever, in a state of intoxication, appears in any public place, or in any place which it is a trespass in him to enter, and there conducts himself i

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 355
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, in a state of intoxication, appears in any public place, or in any place which it is a trespass in him to enter, and there conducts himself i

extend to twenty-four hours, or with fine which may extend to one thousand rupees, or with
both or with community service.
Of defamation
