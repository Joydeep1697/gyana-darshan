# BNS Section 216: Whoever, being legally bound by an oath or affirmation to state the truth on any subject to any public servant or other person authorised by law to ad

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 216
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, being legally bound by an oath or affirmation to state the truth on any subject to any public servant or other person authorised by law to ad

that subject, any statement which is false, and which he either knows or believes to be
false or does not believe to be true, shall be punished with imprisonment of either
description for a term which may extend to three years, and shall also be liable to fine.
