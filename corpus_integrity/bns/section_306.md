# BNS Section 306: Whoever, being a clerk or servant, or being employed in the capacity of a clerk or servant, commits theft in respect of any property in the possession

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 306
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, being a clerk or servant, or being employed in the capacity of a clerk or servant, commits theft in respect of any property in the possession

seven years, and shall also be liable to fine.
