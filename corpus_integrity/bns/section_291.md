# BNS Section 291: Whoever knowingly or negligently omits to take such measures with any animal in his possession as is sufficient to guard against any probable danger t

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 291
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever knowingly or negligently omits to take such measures with any animal in his possession as is sufficient to guard against any probable danger t

either description for a term which may extend to six months, or with fine which may extend
to five thousand rupees, or with both.
