# BNS Section 71: Whoever has been previously convicted of an offence punishable under section 64 or section 65 or section 66 or section 70 and is subsequently convicte

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 71
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever has been previously convicted of an offence punishable under section 64 or section 65 or section 66 or section 70 and is subsequently convicte

life which shall mean imprisonment for the remainder of that person’s natural life, or with
death.
