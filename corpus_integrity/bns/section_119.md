# BNS Section 119: (1)Whoever voluntarily causes hurt for the purpose of extorting from the sufferer, or from any person interested in the sufferer, any property or valu

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 119
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

(1)Whoever voluntarily causes hurt for the purpose of extorting from the sufferer, or from any person interested in the sufferer, any property or valu

illegal or which may facilitate the commission of an offence, shall be punished with
imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to ten years, and shall also be
liable to fine.
(2) Whoever voluntarily causes grievous hurt for any purpose referred to in
sub-section (1), shall be punished with imprisonment for life, or imprisonment of either
description for a term which may extend to ten years, and shall also be liable to fine.
