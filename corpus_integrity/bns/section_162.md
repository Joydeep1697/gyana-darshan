# BNS Section 162: Whoever abets an assault by an officer, soldier, sailor or airman, in the Army, Navy orAir Force of the Government of India, on any superior officer b

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 162
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever abets an assault by an officer, soldier, sailor or airman, in the Army, Navy orAir Force of the Government of India, on any superior officer b

with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to seven years, and shall
also be liable to fine.
