# BNS Section 274: Whoever adulterates any article of food or drink, so as to make such article noxious as food or drink, intending to sell such article as food or drink

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 274
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever adulterates any article of food or drink, so as to make such article noxious as food or drink, intending to sell such article as food or drink

either description for a term which may extend to six months, or with fine which may extend
to five thousand rupees, or with both.
