# BNS Section 73: Whoever prints or publishes any matter in relation to any proceeding before a Court with respect to an offence referred to in section 72 without the p

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 73
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever prints or publishes any matter in relation to any proceeding before a Court with respect to an offence referred to in section 72 without the p

extend to two years and shall also be liable to fine.
Explanation.—The printing or publication of the judgment of any High Court or the
Supreme Court does not amount to an offence within the meaning of this section.
