# BNS Section 166: Whoever abets what he knows to be an act of insubordination by an officer, soldier, sailor or airman, in theArmy, Navy orAir Force, of the Government

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 166
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever abets what he knows to be an act of insubordination by an officer, soldier, sailor or airman, in theArmy, Navy orAir Force, of the Government

with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to two years, or with
fine, or with both.
