# BNS Section 266: Whoever, having accepted any conditional remission of punishment, knowingly violates any condition on which such remission was granted, shall be punis

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 266
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, having accepted any conditional remission of punishment, knowingly violates any condition on which such remission was granted, shall be punis

punishment, and if he has suffered any part of that punishment, then with so much of that
punishment as he has not already suffered.
