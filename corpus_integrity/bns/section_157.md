# BNS Section 157: Whoever, being a public servant and having the custody of any State prisoner or prisoner of war, negligently suffers such prisoner to escape from any

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 157
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, being a public servant and having the custody of any State prisoner or prisoner of war, negligently suffers such prisoner to escape from any

which may extend to three years, and shall also be liable to fine.
