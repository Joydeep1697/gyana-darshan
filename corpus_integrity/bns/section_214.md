# BNS Section 214: Whoever, being legally bound to state the truth on any subject to any public servant, refuses to answer any question demanded of him touching that sub

- Statute: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Short name: BNS
- Section: 214
- Act number: Act 45 of 2023
- Source: Official Gazette of India (Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1, No. 53, Dec 25, 2023)

## Text

Whoever, being legally bound to state the truth on any subject to any public servant, refuses to answer any question demanded of him touching that sub

with simple imprisonment for a term which may extend to six months, or with fine which
may extend to five thousand rupees, or with both.
